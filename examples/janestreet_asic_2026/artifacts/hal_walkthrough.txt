  16 leaf net(s) -> clk      at buffer depth 2
So this is *one* clock domain wearing a clock tree, not 16 domains --
the 32 clkbuf cells in the census are exactly that tree.

No flop has an enable pin: sky130's df*tp family has none, so every
register in this design is gated by a multiplexer in its D cone, not by
a control pin.  Step 2 therefore separates *reset polarity* here and
nothing else -- the enable-group split the method expects on an FPGA
export does not exist in a standard-cell flow.

Result< Report > hal::example_analysis::analyze ( Netlist *  netlist)

Group the sequential gates of a netlist by the net that drives their clock pin.

Errors are returned, never logged-and-swallowed: a caller that gets an empty result must be able to tell "this design has no registers" from "this analysis cannot look at this design". The error message names what was missing and what to do about it.

Parameters
[in]netlist- The netlist to analyze.
Returns
The report on success, an error otherwise.
